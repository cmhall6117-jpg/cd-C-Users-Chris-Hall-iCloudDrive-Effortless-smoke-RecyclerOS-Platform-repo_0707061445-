# VS-002 Vehicle Digital Twin

Capability: Vehicle Intelligence / Vehicle Digital Twin  
Workflows: Opportunity Discovery, Vehicle Receiving, Focus Point Harvest, Inventory Intake, Scrap Closeout  
Events: EVT-002 Vehicle Evaluated, EVT-006 Vehicle Received, EVT-009 Part Harvested, EVT-015 Vehicle Scrapped  
Objects: Vehicle, Opportunity, Harvest Session, Inventory Item, Business Event, GPS Location, Photo/Media  

## Acceptance Criteria
1. User can view a vehicle profile by VIN or vehicle code.
2. Vehicle record displays lifecycle status.
3. Vehicle record displays procurement intent when linked to opportunity.
4. Vehicle timeline shows related business events.
5. Vehicle record supports offline availability and later synchronization.
