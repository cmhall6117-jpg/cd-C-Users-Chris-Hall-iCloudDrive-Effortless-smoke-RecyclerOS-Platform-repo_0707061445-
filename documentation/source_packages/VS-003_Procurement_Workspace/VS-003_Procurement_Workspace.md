# VS-003 Procurement Workspace

Capability: Procurement Intelligence
Workflow: WFL-002 Auction Evaluation & Procurement
Events: EVT-003 Procurement Intent Assigned, EVT-004 Auction Won, EVT-005 Vehicle Purchased
Objects: Opportunity, Vehicle, Procurement, Business Event

Acceptance Criteria:
1. Compare resale, personal use, and part-out scenarios.
2. Distinguish dealer-only vs non-dealer public auctions.
3. Store recommended max bid by procurement intent.
4. Store projected revenue, costs, margin, confidence score.
5. Require human approval before downstream purchase workflow.
